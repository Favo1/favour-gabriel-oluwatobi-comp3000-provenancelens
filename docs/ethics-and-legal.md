# Legal, ethical, social and professional considerations

This is an initial Sprint 0 assessment and must be revisited when datasets, models and project methods are selected. It is not a substitute for University ethics or legal advice.

- **Copyright and licences:** use only datasets, pretrained models and code whose terms permit the intended research use. Record the official source, licence, restrictions and access date in [data-sources.md](data-sources.md). Do not assume that publicly accessible images are free to reuse.
- **Data protection and privacy:** do not scrape or collect new personal images for this project. Prefer appropriately licensed research datasets; minimise access to identifiable content and do not attempt to identify people in images. Reassess UK GDPR and Data Protection Act implications if personal data is encountered.
- **Human participants:** no human-participant study is planned at project initiation. Any future user study, interview or collection of participant data must receive the required University ethics approval before recruitment or data collection.
- **Dual use and misuse:** image-forensics research can be used to assess content but may also inform attempts to evade detectors. Keep the work focused on evaluation and responsible detection; do not publish evasion-oriented functionality.
- **Reliability and social impact:** false positives and false negatives are possible. Report limitations, class balance, dataset bias, uncertainty and calibration. Present outputs as decision support, not definitive proof that an image is real or fake.
- **Professional conduct:** maintain research integrity, cite external and AI-assisted contributions, preserve reproducible records, and follow relevant professional guidance including the BCS Code of Conduct and University policies.
- **Generative AI:** the assessment is designated Assisted Work. Check and declare AI use in line with the COMP3000 brief, maintain the project AI-use log and complete the required signed declaration.

## Initial ethics position

The project will use appropriately licensed research materials and synthetic/generated examples where feasible. No participant recruitment or new collection of personal images is planned at this stage. This position must be reviewed with the supervisor before data acquisition and whenever the scope changes.
